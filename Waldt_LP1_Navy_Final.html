The Myths That Are Keeping You Stuck

The report exposes 7 dangerous beliefs about back and neck pain. Here are three of them.

Myth“I should wait and see if it gets better.”

Acute back problems that go untreated become chronic ones. Research shows pain lasting more than 6 weeks has a dramatically lower rate of spontaneous resolution. Every week you wait, the pattern becomes more entrenched.

Myth“Surgery is probably my only option.”

Between 10 and 40 percent of lumbar spine surgery patients experience “Failed Back Surgery Syndrome.” Research shows most disc herniations can be treated with comprehensive conservative hands-on care. Surgery should be the last door, not the first.

Myth“Pain medication is managing my problem.”

Medication reduces your awareness of the problem — not the problem itself. The restricted joints, fascial tension, and structural imbalances continue untouched. Managing the signal while the cause continues is not treatment — it’s delay.
